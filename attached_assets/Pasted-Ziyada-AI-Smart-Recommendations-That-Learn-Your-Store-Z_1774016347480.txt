Ziyada AI — Smart Recommendations That Learn Your Store
Ziyada now comes with a built-in AI recommendation engine that automatically learns your store's data and starts suggesting the right products to the right customers at the right moment — with zero manual setup required.
The moment you activate Ziyada, our engine analyzes your product catalog, order history, and customer behavior to build a unique intelligence profile for your store. From that point forward, every customer visit becomes an opportunity.
What the AI does:
It tracks which products are frequently bought together and surfaces them as bundles at checkout. It identifies your best-selling products and promotes them to new visitors on the main page. It recognizes when a customer adds a low-value product to their cart and suggests a higher-value alternative that fits their intent. It adapts to seasonal patterns — boosting products that sell more during Ramadan, Eid, or peak seasons. It personalizes recommendations based on the customer's country, device, gender, and shopping history across the platform.
What makes it different:
Most upsell tools show the same products to everyone. Ziyada's AI scores every candidate product across five tiers — from personalized customer history down to store-wide trending — and picks the most relevant one for each specific moment. It suppresses recommendations when they don't make sense, like when the cart is already full or the customer just removed items, so you never annoy your customers with irrelevant popups.
The engine re-trains automatically every night with fresh data, and every time a new order comes in, it gets smarter.
The result: more cart additions, higher average order values, and recommendations that feel natural — not pushy.

بمجرد تفعيل Ziyada، يقوم محركنا بتحليل كتالوج منتجاتك وسجل طلباتك وسلوك عملائك، ليبني ملفاً ذكياً خاصاً بمتجرك. من تلك اللحظة، تصبح كل زيارة فرصة.
ماذا يفعل الذكاء الاصطناعي:

ما الذي يميزه:
