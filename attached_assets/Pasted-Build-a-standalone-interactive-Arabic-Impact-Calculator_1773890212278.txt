Build a standalone interactive Arabic Impact Calculator web page (single HTML file, no frameworks needed).

LANGUAGE: All UI text must be in Arabic (RTL layout).

FUNCTIONALITY:
The calculator has 5 input sliders:

1. الزوار الشهريون (Monthly Visitors) - range: 1,000 to 500,000 - default: 50,000
2. معدل التحويل (Conversion Rate %) - range: 0.5% to 15% - default: 2.5%
3. متوسط قيمة الطلب (Average Order Value) - range: 50 to 5,000 SAR - default: 250 SAR
4. نسبة رفع قيمة الطلب (AOV Uplift %) - range: 10% to 60% - default: 30% - label: "نسبة زيادة قيمة الطلب عبر Upsell/Cross-sell"
5. معدل قبول التوصية (Recommendation Acceptance Rate %) - range: 5% to 50% - default: 20% - label: "نسبة الزبائن الذين يقبلون توصيات المنتجات"

CALCULATION LOGIC:

Base (without recommendations):
- Monthly Orders = Monthly Visitors × (Conversion Rate / 100)
- Revenue = Monthly Orders × AOV

With Cross-sell / Upsell recommendations:
- Accepting Customers = Monthly Orders × (Acceptance Rate / 100)
- Non-accepting Customers = Monthly Orders - Accepting Customers
- Revenue from accepting customers = Accepting Customers × (AOV × (1 + AOV Uplift / 100))
- Revenue from non-accepting customers = Non-accepting Customers × AOV
- New Total Revenue = Revenue from accepting + Revenue from non-accepting
- Effective New AOV = New Total Revenue / Monthly Orders

Impact:
- Additional Revenue = New Total Revenue - Old Revenue (show as +X SAR/month)
- Revenue Growth % = ((New Total Revenue - Old Revenue) / Old Revenue) × 100
- AOV Increase = Effective New AOV - AOV (show as +X SAR)

All values update in real-time as sliders move.

DISPLAY - Two comparison cards:
Card 1 "بدون توصيات":

Card 2 "مع توصيات Cross-sell / Upsell":

Impact section shows:
